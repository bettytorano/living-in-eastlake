Eastlake Buyer's Guide

Before you buy in Eastlake, know these three things

HOAs, Mello-Roos and schools come up in almost every Eastlake purchase. Here's the insider version from someone who has lived here since 1987.

1 · HOAs

Eastlake has three separate HOAs, not one

The Eastlake master-planned community is split into three associations. Each has its own amenities, fees and rules.

Many homes also have a smaller sub-association on top of the master HOA. Always ask for the HOA documents and the total monthly dues before you write an offer.

2 · Mello-Roos

Some original Eastlake neighborhoods no longer pay Mello-Roos

Mello-Roos is a special tax, collected through a Community Facilities District (CFD), that helped pay for Eastlake's schools, roads and infrastructure. It is separate from your HOA dues and your regular property tax.

When we bought in Eastlake Hills in 1987, we paid Mello-Roos for 25 years. Today our neighborhood no longer has it. Newer districts can last longer, so never assume.

How to check any home

  • Look at the property tax bill for line items labeled "CFD" or "Mello-Roos"
  • Ask the agency listed on the bill when the district ends
  • Contact the San Diego County Treasurer-Tax Collector to confirm
  • Or just ask me. I'm happy to look it up with you

3 · Schools

The homes came first. The schools caught up.

Eastlake was promised brand-new neighborhood schools, but families arrived faster than the schools could open. Today the community is served by the Chula Vista Elementary and Sweetwater Union High school districts.

School boundaries can change, so always confirm the assigned school for a specific address with the district.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Does every Eastlake home have Mello-Roos?

No. Whether a home pays Mello-Roos, and how much, depends on the parcel and when it was built. Check the property tax bill for special tax lines. My Mello-Roos in Eastlake guide walks through it step by step.

Are HOA dues and Mello-Roos the same thing?

No. HOA dues are billed by your association and pay for common areas and amenities. Mello-Roos is a special tax collected on your county property tax bill that pays for schools, infrastructure or ongoing public maintenance.

How do I find the HOA dues for an Eastlake home?

Ask for the HOA disclosure documents. In California, sellers in an association must provide them to buyers (Civil Code §4525). Make sure you add the master association and any sub-association together.

When did the Eastlake schools open?

EastLake Elementary opened in 1989, Eastlake High School in 1992, and EastLake Middle School in 2003, about 16 years after the first residents moved in.
