The original Eastlake grocery center

Eastlake Village Center

This is where Eastlake's everyday shopping started. When my family moved here in 1987 there was no grocery store nearby, and the day Vons opened here in 1996 changed daily life for all of us. It sits at the southwest corner of Otay Lakes Road and Eastlake Parkway, and it's still where many of us pick up groceries on the way home.

Eastlake Village Marketplace

Right across Otay Lakes Road from the Village Center, the Marketplace is the big-errand stop: Target and Lowe's, plus banks, a gas station and car wash, a pet hospital and 13 places to eat. Sudberry Properties broke ground on the roughly 410,000-square-foot center in late 2002, and it has served Eastlake for over 20 years.

Village Walk at Eastlake

Village Walk is the prettiest place in Eastlake to run errands. It has winding walkways, bronze statues, a hand-carved stone fountain, a grand clock tower and a big pond full of koi and turtles, plus a sandbox and play area for little ones. Sudberry Properties broke ground in 2006 on the roughly 160,000-square-foot center on Eastlake Parkway, and it opened in 2007.

Fitness, kids' activities and fun

The District at Eastlake

The District is less about shopping and more about things to do. Its motto is “Fit. Family. Fun.” Tucked off Eastlake Parkway near the Eastlake Business Center on Showroom Place, its buildings went up in 2005 and 2006. Today it's the go-to spot for birthday parties, workouts, martial arts and swim lessons, an indoor go-kart track, and events at The Venue.

Walmart, Home Depot and quick bites

Eastlake Terraces

At Olympic Parkway and Eastlake Parkway, right off SR-125, Eastlake Terraces is the largest of the group at about 363,000 square feet. Walmart Supercenter and The Home Depot anchor it, Walgreens is on the corner, and there are drive-thru favorites like Chick-fil-A. It opened in the mid-2000s, and part of the center, The Shops at Eastlake Terraces, changed owners in 2022.

Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do Eastlake residents shop?

Eastlake has five shopping centers within a few minutes of each other: Eastlake Village Center (Vons and Total Wine & More), Eastlake Village Marketplace (Target and Lowe's), Village Walk at Eastlake (Trader Joe's, Sprouts and T.J. Maxx), Eastlake Terraces (Walmart Supercenter and The Home Depot) and The District at Eastlake for fitness and kids' activities. Otay Ranch Town Center, an outdoor mall with dining and a movie theater, is just south.

Is there a Trader Joe's in Eastlake?

Yes. Trader Joe's is at Village Walk at Eastlake, 878 Eastlake Parkway, in the same center as Sprouts Farmers Market, T.J. Maxx, Five Below, Petco and CVS.

Where are Target and Lowe's in Eastlake?

Both anchor Eastlake Village Marketplace at 2275 Otay Lakes Road, at the corner of Eastlake Parkway, just off State Route 125.

Where are Walmart and Home Depot in Eastlake?

Both anchor Eastlake Terraces at Olympic Parkway and Eastlake Parkway, next to State Route 125. The Walmart Supercenter is at 1360 Eastlake Parkway.

When did shopping come to Eastlake?

Eastlake's first homes were built in the late 1980s, but the first grocery store didn't come until Vons opened at Eastlake Village Center in 1996. Eastlake Village Marketplace followed in the early 2000s, Eastlake Terraces and The District in the mid-2000s, and Village Walk at Eastlake opened in 2007.

What is there for kids to do at The District at Eastlake?

The District at Eastlake on Showroom Place has Sky Zone, Speed Circuit indoor go-karts, Play City, Ninja Factory, Floaties Swim School, Pride Martial Arts and Kid Ventures, and several of them offer birthday party packages.
