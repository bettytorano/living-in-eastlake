A Complete Guide to the Eastlake HOAs and Amenities in Eastlake, Chula Vista, CA

By Betty Torano, REALTOR® · Eastlake resident since 1987 · December 10, 2025

The master-planned community of Eastlake in Chula Vista was designed to offer an exceptional lifestyle with beautiful neighborhoods, scenic surroundings and thoughtfully curated amenities. One of the things that makes Eastlake so appealing is the way the community is organized into three distinct homeowners associations, each serving specific neighborhoods and offering its own amenities that support an active, outdoor lifestyle.

Whether you are exploring Eastlake for the first time or already live here and want to understand the HOA structure more clearly, this guide walks you through all three Eastlake HOAs and what each one provides.

Eastlake HOA I

Serves: Eastlake Hills and Eastlake Shores

As the original HOA in Eastlake, HOA I offers some of the most iconic features of the community. With peaceful lakeside views, parks and gathering spaces and a classic Eastlake feel, this HOA is known for its charm and established beauty.

Amenities in Eastlake HOA I

  • Eastlake Beach Club and Pavilion
  • Eastlake Lagoon and Sand Volleyball Courts
  • Eastlake Swim and Tennis Club
  • Eastlake Parks and Playgrounds
  • Eastlake Paddleboats
  • Eastlake Lake Walking Path
  • Eastlake Shorebird Pool

This HOA captures the original vision of Eastlake, blending recreation with natural surroundings, waterside relaxation and beautiful community gathering spaces.

Eastlake HOA II

Serves: Eastlake Greens

Eastlake Greens is known for golf course views, rolling greenbelts and an active lifestyle. HOA II supports this with amenities that encourage recreation, fitness and outdoor enjoyment.

Amenities in Eastlake HOA II

  • Eastlake Tennis and Swim Club (including pickleball courts)
  • Eastlake Dolphin Beach
  • Augusta Park and Pool
  • Eastlake Parks and Playgrounds

Public amenities located in Eastlake Greens

  • Enagic Golf Club at Eastlake
  • Sunset View Park
  • Chula Vista Community Park

This HOA is ideal for residents who enjoy golf, sports and a vibrant, active community.

Eastlake HOA III

Serves: Eastlake Trails, Eastlake Trails North, The Woods at Eastlake and Eastlake Vistas

HOA III covers the easternmost neighborhoods of Eastlake and offers some of the most scenic and expansive amenities. This area is known for its hillside views, large parks and beautiful recreation centers.

Amenities in Eastlake HOA III

  • Eastlake Creekside Clubhouse
  • Eastlake Woods Clubhouse
  • Eastlake Parks and Playgrounds
  • Eastlake Pond

Public amenities located in Eastlake III neighborhoods

  • Eastlake Little League Fields
  • Salt Creek Park and Soccer Arena
  • Centennial Trail
  • Mountain Hawk Park
  • Chula Vista Elite Athlete Training Center
  • Upper Otay Reservoir Trailhead

With its miles of trails, open spaces and recreational facilities, HOA III is a great fit for nature lovers and anyone who enjoys being outdoors.

Understanding Eastlake's Mello-Roos

Because Eastlake is a master-planned community, it was assessed with Mello-Roos special taxes to help fund essential community services and infrastructure. These assessments vary by neighborhood and by parcel. See my Mello-Roos in Eastlake guide to learn how to check any home, or ask me and I'll help you look it up.

Why Buyers Love Eastlake's HOA Structure

The three-HOA system allows Eastlake to maintain beautiful amenities and a consistently high quality of life across all neighborhoods. No matter which part of Eastlake you live in, you have access to well-maintained pools, parks, community centers and scenic spaces.
