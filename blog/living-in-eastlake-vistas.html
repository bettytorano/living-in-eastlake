Living in Eastlake Vistas, Chula Vista: Views, Parks and the Otay Lakes

By Betty Torano, REALTOR® · Eastlake resident since 1987 · October 11, 2026

Eastlake Vistas sits along Eastlake's eastern hills near Mountain Hawk Park and the Otay Lakes, with scenic views, outdoor recreation and newer construction.

As an Eastlake resident since 1987 and a Realtor® who works in every Eastlake neighborhood, I've watched Eastlake Vistas take shape. Here's what to know if you're thinking about buying or selling here.

Where Is Eastlake Vistas?

Eastlake Vistas is on the eastern edge of Eastlake, bounded by Otay Lakes Road to the north, Old Janal Ranch Road to the west and Wueste Road to the east, with a small section just south of Olympic Parkway. Mountain Hawk Park and the Lower Otay Reservoir are close by.

Boundary is approximate. Ask me about a specific address.

The Eastlake III Association

Every home here belongs to the Eastlake III master association, which covers Eastlake Trails, Trails North, The Woods and Eastlake Vistas. Its amenities include:

  • Eastlake Creekside Clubhouse
  • Eastlake Woods Clubhouse
  • Eastlake Pond
  • Multiple pools, fitness spaces, parks, playgrounds and scenic trails

Some homes, especially condos, townhomes and gated communities, also belong to a smaller sub-association with its own dues. Always add the two together when you compare homes. See my complete guide to the Eastlake HOAs and amenities.

Views and the Outdoors

Mountain Hawk Park and the Otay Lakes are right nearby, and the hills around the neighborhood give many homes wide views. The Eastlake Little League Juniors Field on Olympic Vista Road is also in the Vistas, home to the league's Juniors and Challenger divisions.

A Note on Property Taxes

Eastlake III neighborhoods were built later than the original Hills and Shores, so their Mello-Roos districts generally have more years left. Eastlake Vistas is also in the City of Chula Vista's CFD 07M, a maintenance district that funds upkeep of public landscaping and open space in the Woods and Vistas area. Amounts and end dates depend on the specific home.

The only way to know is to check the property tax bill for the specific address. My Mello-Roos in Eastlake guide shows you how in about ten minutes, or send me the address and I'll look it up with you.

Schools

Elementary schools are in the Chula Vista Elementary School District, and middle and high schools are in the Sweetwater Union High School District. Attendance boundaries can change, so confirm the assigned schools for any address with each district.

Who Is Eastlake Vistas Right For?

The Vistas is a great fit for anyone who loves the outdoors, wants views and appreciates newer construction close to the Otay Lakes.
