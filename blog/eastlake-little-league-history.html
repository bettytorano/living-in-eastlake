The History of Eastlake Little League in Chula Vista, CA

By Betty Torano, REALTOR® · Eastlake resident since 1987 · December 8, 2025

If you live in Eastlake or anywhere in Chula Vista, you already know that Eastlake Little League is more than a youth sports program. It is a community tradition, a source of pride and a place where neighbors form lasting connections.

As a local Realtor, longtime Eastlake resident and former Eastlake Little League board member, I've seen firsthand how meaningful this organization is. Here is the story of how it grew into the beloved program it is today.

Before the fields: baseball on empty lots

When our family first moved to Eastlake in 1987, there was no local Little League, so my older son played T-ball at Sweetwater Valley Little League.

After Eastlake Little League was established, teams played on temporary fields scattered throughout the community, including a lot where the Eastlake Post Office stands today.

How it all began

Eastlake Little League was officially founded in 1998 to serve the growing number of families moving into the master-planned Eastlake community. The vision was clear: create a youth baseball program where children could learn the game, develop character and build strong community ties.

One of the most significant moments in the league's early history was the construction of its home fields. The land was generously donated by the EastLake Development Company, and a dedicated group of parents stepped forward to help bring the project to life.

My husband and I were proud to be among those parents, serving on the board during the construction and development of the fields. It was a true community effort that reflected the spirit of Eastlake. Everyone worked together to create a beautiful, well-organized place for our children to play and grow.

A legacy of excellence: home of the 2013 U.S. Little League Champions

Eastlake Little League earned national recognition in 2013, when the league's All-Star team became the United States Champions at the Little League World Series in Williamsport, Pennsylvania. It was an unforgettable moment for Chula Vista and a proud highlight in the league's history.

The achievement brought Eastlake into the national spotlight and inspired young players throughout the community. Many people still remember gathering to watch the televised games and cheering as the team represented Eastlake and the spirit of Chula Vista baseball.

A program built on community and volunteerism

From the beginning, Eastlake Little League has been powered by volunteers. Parents serve as coaches, managers, umpires and board members. Local businesses sponsor teams and events. Neighbors fill the stands to support every player on the field.

That level of involvement has made Eastlake Little League one of the most respected programs in District 42 and throughout San Diego County.

The fields that bring the community together

The Eastlake Little League complex in Eastlake Trails, built on donated land and shaped by community dedication, remains one of the finest youth baseball facilities in the region. People come together here for Opening Day ceremonies, spring and fall league games, All-Star competitions, team celebrations and community events.

The smell of fresh-cut grass, the cheers from the stands and the energy of young athletes make these fields a place where memories are made. Many players begin in T-ball, move through Minors, Majors and Juniors, and later return as mentors, coaches or volunteers.

A source of pride for Eastlake and beyond

The league's success has helped shape Eastlake's identity. Many former players continue their baseball careers at Eastlake High School, which has its own strong baseball tradition. Others go on to college programs, and a few have moved into professional baseball, carrying the sportsmanship and community values they learned on these fields.
