Our First Eastlake Home: $132,000 at a 10.25% Interest Rate

By Betty Torano, REALTOR® · Eastlake resident since 1987 · September 19, 2026

Our first Eastlake home cost $132,000. Our mortgage interest rate? 10.25%.

What $132,000 bought in 1987

In 1987 we purchased a three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath home with about 1,800 square feet, in the final phase of our neighborhood.

Why each phase cost more

Builders released Eastlake homes in phases, and prices increased with each new release. Buyers knew that if they missed one phase, they could pay more in the next. That's a big part of why some families camped out overnight for the opportunity to buy.

Then and now

Interest rates and prices have changed a lot since then, and so has Eastlake. If you bought in the early years, you've likely built significant equity. If you're curious what your home is worth today, I'm happy to prepare a free, no-pressure home value based on recent sales near you.
