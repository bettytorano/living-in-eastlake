At a glance

What Is Eastlake?

Eastlake is a master-planned community on the east side of Chula Vista, in southern San Diego County. The first homes were built in the mid-1980s around a private lake, and the community grew east toward the hills and the Otay Lakes over the next 25 years.

Today Eastlake has seven residential neighborhoods in ZIP codes 91913, 91914 and 91915, three master homeowners associations, its own shopping centers, parks and trails, and schools right in the community.

Good to know before you visit

  • Eastlake is part of the City of Chula Vista, not a separate city
  • Most homes belong to an HOA, and some also pay Mello-Roos
  • State Route 125 (a toll road) runs right through the community
  • Weather is mild year-round, a little warmer than the coast in summer

Monthly costs

What a Home Really Costs Each Month

The price is only part of the story. Before you fall in love with a house, add up these four costs. I'll line them up for any home you're considering.

1. Property tax

In California, the base tax is 1% of your purchase price, plus local voter-approved charges. It's reassessed when you buy, then can rise by no more than 2% a year under Proposition 13.

2. HOA dues

Eastlake has three master HOAs, and many homes also belong to a smaller sub-association. Always ask for the total monthly dues. How Eastlake's HOAs work →

3. Mello-Roos and special taxes

Some Eastlake homes pay Mello-Roos, a special tax that helped build the schools and roads. Others have paid it off. It depends on the address, so we check the tax bill for every home. Mello-Roos in Eastlake →

4. Homeowners insurance

Get insurance quotes early, as soon as you're serious about a home. Insurance has become harder to find in parts of California, and your lender will need a policy in place before closing.

Insider tip: Lenders count HOA dues and Mello-Roos in your monthly budget, so two homes at the same price can qualify very differently. Knowing these numbers up front avoids surprises.

Life in Eastlake

What You'll Enjoy Here

The lake and HOA clubs

The Eastlake lake, Beach Club and lagoon, plus pools, tennis and pickleball clubs and clubhouses run by each HOA for its residents.

Parks and trails

Mountain Hawk Park, Salt Creek Park, the Centennial Trail, and the Otay Lakes on the community's eastern edge.

Golf and sports

Enagic Golf Club at Eastlake, the Eastlake Little League fields, and the Elite Athlete Training Center, the former U.S. Olympic Training Center.

Shopping and dining

Eastlake Village Center, Eastlake Village Marketplace and Village Walk for everyday errands, and Otay Ranch Town Center just south.

Beaches and the bay

Imperial Beach and Coronado are about 20–30 minutes away, and the Chula Vista Bayfront is closer still.

Easy trips south

The Otay Mesa border crossing is about 15 minutes away for trips into Baja California.

Buying with a VA loan

  • No VA loan limit if you have full entitlement, so you may be able to buy with no down payment
  • Get your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) and a pre-approval from a lender experienced with VA loans
  • Condos and townhomes need to be in a VA-approved project, so we check that first
  • Veterans with a service-connected disability may be exempt from the VA funding fee
  • HOA dues and Mello-Roos count toward what you qualify for, so we confirm them early

Buying from out of town

  • Live video tours of homes and the neighborhood, so you can shop before you arrive

Your moving plan

Relocation Timeline: From Orders to Keys

Every move is different, but this is the order I walk my out-of-town buyers through.

  • As soon as you knowCall me and a lender. Get pre-approved (and your COE for a VA loan), set a budget that includes HOA dues and Mello-Roos, and check your BAH if you're military.
  • 60–90 days outNarrow down neighborhoods together. I'll send homes that fit, including ones coming up soon, and we'll start video tours.
  • 30–60 days outMake an offer. Escrow often takes about 30–45 days. Inspections, the appraisal and HOA documents happen during this time.
  • Closing weekFinal walk-through (in person or by video), sign the loan documents, and get your keys.
  • After you arriveEnroll in school with the district, set up utilities, and get to know your HOA's clubs and amenities.
PCS tip: If your report date and closing date don't line up, plan for temporary lodging. Ask your installation about Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) and keep your receipts.
Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Eastlake from downtown San Diego?

About 16 miles, roughly a 20 to 25 minute drive without traffic via State Route 125 and State Route 54 or Interstate 805. Weekday rush hour can add a lot, so test your commute at the time you would actually drive it.

How far is Eastlake from the San Diego military bases?

Without traffic, Naval Base San Diego is about 20 minutes away, Naval Amphibious Base Coronado and MCAS Miramar about 30 minutes, Naval Air Station North Island about 30 to 35 minutes, and Camp Pendleton about an hour. Morning traffic can add 15 to 30 minutes or more.

Can I use a VA loan to buy in Eastlake?

Yes. With full VA entitlement there is no VA loan limit, so many buyers purchase with no down payment. Condos and townhomes must be in a VA-approved project, and lenders count HOA dues and Mello-Roos in your monthly budget, so confirm those early.

Can I buy a home in Eastlake before I move to San Diego?

Yes. I tour homes with out-of-town buyers by live video, and offers, disclosures and most escrow paperwork are signed electronically. Escrow often takes about 30 to 45 days.

What should I budget for besides the mortgage?

Property tax (a base of 1% of the purchase price plus local charges), HOA dues for the master association and any sub-association, Mello-Roos or other special taxes if the home has them, and homeowners insurance.

Which school districts serve Eastlake?

The Chula Vista Elementary School District for grades K–6 and the Sweetwater Union High School District for grades 7–12. Boundaries can change, so confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with each district.
