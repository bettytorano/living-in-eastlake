Meet Betty

I didn't just study Eastlake. I've lived it.

When my family moved to Eastlake Hills in 1987, EastLake Elementary hadn't opened yet, our kids played Little League on empty lots, and people camped out overnight for the chance to buy in the next phase.

Nearly 40 years later, I'm still here, and I help my neighbors and newcomers make smart moves in the community I love. I know which neighborhoods still carry Mello-Roos, how the three HOAs differ, and what really drives value from one street to the next.

Life in Eastlake

"City close, country quiet"

The original Eastlake motto still fits. Here are a few local favorites.

Outdoors

Trails & open space

Mother Miguel Mountain, Sweetwater Reservoir trails and Mackenzie Creek Park, plus the lake views Eastlake is known for.

Sports

Play and train

Eastlake Little League fields in the Trails and the Elite Athlete Training Center (the former Olympic Training Center) next door.

Entertainment

Shows, shops & nature

Concerts at North Island Credit Union Amphitheatre, Otay Ranch Town Center, and the Living Coast Discovery Center.
