Why Eastlake residents love living here

“City Close, Country Quiet”

The original Eastlake motto still fits. Nearly 40 years in, these are the reasons neighbors give me most often.

  1. 01

    Master-planned design

    Thoughtfully developed neighborhoods with beautiful landscaping and a strong sense of community.

  2. 02

    Resort-style amenities

    Multiple pools, parks, playgrounds and private clubhouses, depending on your HOA.

  3. 03

    Schools inside the community

    Several elementary schools plus EastLake Middle and Eastlake High, served by the Chula Vista Elementary and Sweetwater Union High districts.

  4. 04

    Shopping and dining minutes away

    Eastlake Village Center, Eastlake Village Marketplace, Village Walk and Otay Ranch Town Center.

  5. 05

    Parks, dog parks and community events

    Welcoming neighborhoods with open spaces, dog parks and events throughout the year.

  6. 06

    Trails, open space and lake views

    Lakeside walks, peaceful parks and well-kept greenbelts.

  7. 07

    Golf close to home

    Tee off at the Enagic Golf Club at Eastlake, right in Eastlake Greens.

  8. 08

    Hiking and outdoor adventure

    Trails and hillsides for hiking and biking, from Mother Miguel Mountain to the Otay Lakes.

  9. 09

    Next to an Olympic-level training center

    The Elite Athlete Training Center, the former U.S. Olympic Training Center, is just down the road.

  10. 10

    Close to employers and medical care

    Easy access to healthcare centers, professional offices and major employers.
