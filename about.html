Meet Betty

I didn't just study Eastlake. I've lived it.

San Diego has been home since I was ten, when my father retired from the U.S. Army. When my family moved to Eastlake Hills in 1987, EastLake Elementary hadn't opened yet, our kids played Little League on empty lots, and people camped out overnight for the chance to buy in the next phase.

Before real estate, I earned a math degree from SDSU and worked as a software engineer at SAIC. Later I owned White Magnolia Home, an interior decor and furniture store right here in the Eastlake Business Center, where I also offered home staging to local agents. I've been a licensed REALTOR® since 2013, and I bring all of it to my clients: a designer's eye, a love of technology and digital marketing, and nearly 40 years of Eastlake knowledge.

I know which neighborhoods still carry Mello-Roos, how the three HOAs differ, and what really drives value from one street to the next.
