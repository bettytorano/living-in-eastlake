What Schools Serve Eastlake, Chula Vista?

Four elementary schools, a middle school and a high school sit inside or right beside Eastlake's neighborhoods. Here's where each one is, what grades it serves and when it opened, plus charter, private and preschool options nearby.

By Betty Torano, REALTOR® · Eastlake resident since 1987 · Last reviewed November 24, 2026

How Eastlake's schools grew

Families First, Then the Schools

When my family moved to Eastlake in 1987, there wasn't a single school in the community yet. The schools came one by one as the neighborhoods filled in, and the middle school took until 2003. Read the full story →

Two public school districts serve Eastlake today: the Chula Vista Elementary School District for kindergarten through sixth grade, and the Sweetwater Union High School District for grades 7 through 12.

Elementary Schools

All four are part of the Chula Vista Elementary School District. Which one a home is assigned to depends on the address, so check with the district before you buy or rent.

EastLake Elementary School

Eastlake's first school, on Hillside Drive in Eastlake Hills. It opened in 1989, two years after the first families moved in, and many homes in the Hills are within walking distance.

Home of the Titans

Eastlake High School

Eastlake High opened in September 1992, the moment high schoolers no longer had to leave the community for school. Home of the Titans, it's a four-year comprehensive high school on Eastlake Parkway, next to Eastlake Greens. It offers college-prep (A–G) courses, Spanish, French, Filipino and Japanese, career pathways such as arts and media and health science, and music and drama in the Chapman Center for the Performing Arts. Its marching band, the Titan Regiment, is a fixture at local competitions.

Before you buy or rent

How to Confirm the School for an Address

School assignments follow district boundaries, and those can change when new schools open. I always recommend confirming directly with the districts for the exact address you're considering.

I share facts about schools, not rankings. For official test results and other measures, see the California School Dashboard.

  1. Grades K–6: contact the Chula Vista Elementary School District with the property address.
  2. Grades 7–12: contact the Sweetwater Union High School District.
  3. Visit: call the school office to ask about tours, programs and before- and after-school care.
  4. Taxes: some Eastlake homes pay a Mello-Roos special tax that helped build these schools. See my Mello-Roos guide →
Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What school districts serve Eastlake, Chula Vista?

Eastlake is served by the Chula Vista Elementary School District for kindergarten through sixth grade and the Sweetwater Union High School District for grades 7 through 12.

What high school do Eastlake students attend?

Eastlake High School, home of the Titans, at 1120 Eastlake Parkway. It opened in 1992 and serves grades 9–12. Attendance boundaries can change, so confirm the assigned high school for a specific address with the Sweetwater Union High School District.

What middle school serves Eastlake?

EastLake Middle School at 900 Duncan Ranch Road, which opened in 2003 and serves grades 7 and 8. Before it opened, Eastlake's middle school students attended schools outside the community.

What elementary schools are in Eastlake?

EastLake Elementary (1989), Olympic View Elementary (1995), Arroyo Vista Charter (1999) and Salt Creek Elementary (2004), all in the Chula Vista Elementary School District.

How do I find the assigned school for an Eastlake address?

Contact the Chula Vista Elementary School District for grades K–6 and the Sweetwater Union High School District for grades 7–12. Each district can confirm the school assigned to a specific address. Boundaries change as new schools open, so check before you buy or rent.

Do Eastlake homeowners pay Mello-Roos for schools?

Many do. The Sweetwater Union High School District's Community Facilities District No. 1 helped pay for Eastlake's schools, and some homes still pay that special tax. Whether a home pays depends on the property, so check its tax bill. My Mello-Roos guide explains how.
