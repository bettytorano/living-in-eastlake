Eastlake History

Eastlake, as I remember it

I moved to Eastlake in 1987, during the final phase of our neighborhood. These are the stories I share with neighbors, new and old.

Our History

  1. Mid-1980s

    Eastlake Breaks Ground

    Chula Vista’s first master-planned community begins with Eastlake Hills and Eastlake Shores, bringing something South County had rarely seen: a man-made lake, Beach Club, lagoon and Swim & Tennis Club.

    Read the story →
  2. Late 1980s

    Buyers Camp Out Overnight

    Homes are released in phases and prices rise with each release, so buyers line up overnight for the chance to buy in the next phase.

    Read the story →
  3. 1987

    Our Family Moves to Eastlake Hills

    We buy a three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath home of about 1,800 square feet in the final phase of our neighborhood, for $132,000 at a 10.25% interest rate.

    Read the story →
  4. 1989

    EastLake Elementary Opens

    The community’s first neighborhood school opens. Families had arrived faster than the schools could keep up.

    Read the story →
  5. 1992

    Eastlake High School Opens

    High school students no longer have to leave the community, a milestone for a fast-growing Eastlake.

  6. 1995

    Olympic View Elementary Opens

    A second elementary school opens in Eastlake Greens.

    Read the story →
  7. 1998

    Eastlake Little League Is Founded

    After years of playing on temporary lots, including where the Eastlake Post Office stands today, the league gets permanent fields in Eastlake Trails on land donated by the EastLake Development Company.

    Read the story →
  8. 1999

    Arroyo Vista Charter School Opens

    A K–6 charter school, now K–8, opens in Eastlake Trails.

    Read the story →
  9. 2003

    EastLake Middle School Opens

    Nearly 16 years after the first residents moved in, the community’s middle school opens.

    Read the story →
  10. 2004

    Salt Creek Elementary Opens

    Another elementary school opens to serve Eastlake's eastern neighborhoods.

    Read the story →
  11. 2013

    U.S. Little League Champions

    Eastlake’s All-Star team becomes the United States Champions at the Little League World Series in Williamsport, Pennsylvania.

    Read the story →
  12. Today

    Still Home

    Many original neighborhoods have paid off their Mello-Roos, and nearly 40 years later I’m still here, sharing Eastlake’s story with neighbors new and old.

    Read the story →
