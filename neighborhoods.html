Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What neighborhoods are in Eastlake, Chula Vista?

Eastlake's residential neighborhoods are Eastlake Hills, Eastlake Shores, Eastlake Greens, Eastlake Trails, Eastlake Trails North, The Woods and Eastlake Vistas. The community also includes the Eastlake Village Center and Business Center.

How many HOAs does Eastlake have?

Three master associations. Eastlake I covers the Shores and Hills, Eastlake II covers the Greens, and Eastlake III covers the Trails, Trails North, Vistas and the Woods. Many homes also belong to a smaller sub-association, so always ask for the total monthly dues.

Do all Eastlake neighborhoods pay Mello-Roos?

No. It depends on the parcel. Some original neighborhoods have paid off their school Mello-Roos, while city open space maintenance charges, which are assessments rather than Mello-Roos, are designed to continue. See my Mello-Roos in Eastlake guide for how to check any home.

Which school districts serve Eastlake?

Eastlake is served by the Chula Vista Elementary School District for elementary grades and the Sweetwater Union High School District for middle and high school. Boundaries can change, so confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with each district.
